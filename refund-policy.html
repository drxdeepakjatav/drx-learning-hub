Refund & Cancellation Policy

How payments, cancellations and refunds work at DRx Learning Hub.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Our courses are digital products (PDF study material, videos, notes and tests) that open in your account as soon as your payment is confirmed. Please read this policy before you pay.

1. Check before you buy

The first units of every paid course are free. Please open them and check that the course suits you before you pay.

2. Cancellation

Once a course is unlocked in your account, the order cannot be cancelled, because the digital content is available to you immediately.

3. Refunds

Because of the nature of digital content, payments are generally not refundable after the course is unlocked. We will refund or correct your payment in these cases:

  • Double payment: you were charged more than once for the same course.
  • Payment deducted but course not unlocked: your money was taken but the course did not open and we are unable to unlock it.
  • Technical problem from our side that stops you from opening the paid content, and we cannot fix it.

4. How to ask for a refund

  • Contact us within 7 days of the payment using our Contact page.
  • Choose the subject "Course Enrollment" or "Technical Problem" and write your registered email, course name and payment reference (UTR / payment ID).
  • We will check your request and reply with the decision.

5. Refund time

An approved refund is sent to the same account or payment method that was used to pay. It usually reaches you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Failed payments

If a payment fails but the amount is deducted from your account, the bank or payment provider normally returns it automatically within a few working days. If it does not, please contact us with your payment details.

7. Contact

For any question about payments or refunds, please use our Contact page.
