About DRx Learning Hub

Learn • Practice • Prepare • Grow

Who we are

DRx Learning Hub is an educational platform that gives students organized study material, video lectures, short notes and online tests in one place. Our goal is to make learning simple, accessible and exam-oriented.

What you get

  • Unit wise study material: PDF notes arranged by category, semester or year, course and unit.
  • Video lectures: lectures and course playlists on our YouTube channel.
  • Notes: short notes and unit notes for quick revision.
  • Online tests: MCQ tests to practice and check your preparation, with your results saved in your profile.

How it works

  • 1. Create a free account and choose the course you want to join.
  • 2. Start learning. The first units of every paid course are free, so you can check the quality before you enroll.
  • 3. Enroll in the full course to open all units, then practice with notes and tests.

Watch and follow us

New lectures are uploaded on our YouTube channel and appear on the Latest Videos page automatically.
