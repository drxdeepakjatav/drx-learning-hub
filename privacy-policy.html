Privacy Policy

How DRx Learning Hub collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Welcome to DRx Learning Hub ("we", "us", "our"). This website provides study material, video lectures, notes and online tests for students. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password when you create a student account. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We cannot see or read it.
  • Profile and learning data: your account ID, role, registration date, the courses you are enrolled in, and your test attempts and results (such as scores).
  • Payment information: paid courses are paid for directly through UPI. We do not collect or store your card, bank or UPI PIN details. We may keep the payment reference (such as a UTR number) or screenshot you send us so that we can confirm your payment and enroll you.
  • Messages: the name, email, subject and message you send us through the Contact page.
  • Technical information: basic data such as IP address, browser type and device that our hosting and service providers receive automatically when you visit the website.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your student account and log you in.
  • To show you only the courses, units, notes and tests you are enrolled in.
  • To record and show your test results.
  • To reply to your questions and support requests.
  • To keep the website secure and to fix technical problems.
  • To improve our courses and content.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

We use trusted services to run the website. They may process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store accounts, courses, enrollments, units and results.
  • Cloudflare Pages to host and deliver the website.
  • YouTube to play video lectures and to show the latest videos of our channel. Videos are embedded using YouTube's privacy enhanced mode. Public services may be used to read our public YouTube channel feed.
  • Google Drive to display PDF study material.

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage only for what the website needs to work, for example to keep you logged in and to remember a short list of our latest YouTube videos so pages load faster. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear this data anytime from your browser settings; you will simply need to log in again.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and for record keeping.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure connections (HTTPS), password handling by Firebase Authentication and access rules that limit who can read your data. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information and account at any time. Please contact us from the email address linked with your account so we can verify your request.

8. Children's privacy

Our website is meant for students and learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the "Last updated" date at the top.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please use our Contact page.
